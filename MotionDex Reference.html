MotionDex — Technical Reference

Body Position, Actigraphy
& Respiratory Effort

Reference for every metric MotionDex surfaces from Polar inertial streams (H10 chest strap, Verity Sense armband) — body position from the gravity vector, actigraphy from de-gravitated acceleration, and respiratory rate and effort from the chest stream. Definitions, formulas, coverage rules, and the evidence base behind each. Companion to the analyzer; not a substitute for clinical evaluation.

⚠️
Important: body-position labels are a convention. MotionDex classifies posture from the gravity vector in the raw, uncalibrated device frame — the gravity angles are measured, but the posture names are not device-validated, and the left/right sign is ungrounded. Respiratory effort is an accelerometer surrogate, not a flow or pressure measurement.
Evidence Measured Validated Emerging Experimental Heuristic fill = trust · hover a badge for source
ℹ️
No sleep staging here. MotionDex is deliberately single-signal. Sleep/wake staging and apnea typing are Integrator fusions that consume this node’s export — they are not MotionDex metrics. What MotionDex publishes, it publishes with its coverage: an uncovered epoch is absence (null), never stillness.

Body Position
Gravity-vector dwell per 30 s epoch · uncalibrated device frame

Each 30 s epoch, MotionDex takes the component medians of the epoch’s acceleration samples as the gravity vector, in g. A fixed-threshold, Z-first scheme then names the posture. Position prefers the chest (torso) stream and falls back to the wrist stream. An epoch whose gravity magnitude falls outside 0.4–2.0 g is not static, so it reads unknown; an epoch with no samples at all leaves the dwell denominator entirely — it is neither a posture nor an unknown posture, although the timeline still records it as unknown so the track stays dense.

Epoch classRule (gravity medians, unit vector u)Read
supine|uz| ≥ 0.7 and gz > 0Back down, sensor facing up
prone|uz| ≥ 0.7 and gz < 0Front down
upright|uy| ≥ 0.55Torso vertical — a wake / out-of-bed signal
left / rightotherwise, by the sign of gxLateral — the X sign is ungrounded in an uncalibrated frame
unknownmagnitude outside 0.4–2.0 gMoving or off-body — counted in the track, never in a dwell share
Supine timeSupine Dwell Share
Core

The share of covered epochs classified supine — the dwell that positional sleep-apnea management aims to reduce, which is why this is the one posture share the registry promotes to a metric.

Formula
supineFrac = supine epochs / covered epochs (30 s epochs; uncovered epochs excluded)
No fixed clinical cut-point — read it against the positional target set for the individual. The share is computed exactly as stated; the posture label is a convention (uncalibrated frame).
Upright timeUpright Dwell Share
Advanced

Declared in the registry, not implemented. No compute site exists, so this metric reaches no export and no surface. The declaration records the intent: gravity-vector upright dwell as a coarse wake / out-of-bed proxy. The position track above does classify upright epochs; a registered upright share does not exist yet.

No formula is stated, because no computation exists to state one for. This card documents the registry declaration itself.
Lateral timeLateral Dwell Share
Research

Declared in the registry, not implemented. No compute site exists, so this metric reaches no export and no surface. The declaration records the intent: combined left/right gravity-vector dwell. The position track above does classify lateral epochs; a registered lateral share does not exist yet, and the left/right sign it would rest on is ungrounded in the uncalibrated frame.

No formula is stated, because no computation exists to state one for. This card documents the registry declaration itself.
ℹ️
Why the two dormant declarations carry cards. Upright time and Lateral time are declared in the MotionDex registry but not implemented — no compute site exists, so they reach no export and no surface. The guide documents the full registry, so each declaration gets a card that states its status instead of a formula. The app’s dwell bar still shows every epoch class from the position track above; only the supine share is a live registered metric.

Actigraphy
De-gravitated activity counts per 30 s epoch · native measured rate

Actigraphy works on the dynamic part of the signal: a ≈1 s moving-average baseline removes gravity, and what remains is movement. Sampling rate is always the measured native rate (the median inter-sample interval) — never a nominal rate and never count ÷ span. Epochs inside a recorded blanking run (the ACCRUNS sidecar) are excluded, and an uncovered epoch is not recording — its count and its moving flag are both null, never immobile.

Activity countsTotal Dynamic Acceleration
Advanced

The night’s total movement, summed over covered epochs — the raw actigraphic volume the other two actigraphy metrics normalise.

Formula
epoch count = Σ dyn over the 30 s epoch, dyn = |mag − gravity baseline| samples with dyn ≤ 0.02 g contribute 0; totalCounts = Σ epoch counts (covered epochs)
Direct statistic — no published clinical cut-point; compare across your own nights and devices with care (the sum scales with the measured sampling rate).
Movement indexMean Per-Epoch Activity
Core

Average activity per covered epoch — the restlessness headline. Lower is more restful.

Formula
movementIndex = totalCounts / coveredEpochs
Relative measure — no fixed clinical cut-point; a magnitude, not a span, so it is still published when a clock seam refuses the immobile fraction below.
Immobile timeImmobile Epoch Share
Core

The share of covered epochs at or below the movement threshold — stillness, stated only where stillness was actually observed.

Formula
an epoch is moving iff its count > 1.0; immobileFrac = immobile epochs / covered epochs
If the stream carries a device-clock resync or a placed recorded seam, immobileFrac is refused — published as null with the reason “clock-seam” — because a seam makes span-based shares untrustworthy. The refusal is the honest output, not a failure.
Direct statistic over covered epochs only — uncovered epochs never enter the denominator, and are never read as immobile.

Respiratory Effort & Rate
Chest-ACC thoraco-abdominal surrogate · spectral ridge tracking

Breathing lives on the chest stream alone. A 10 s moving average removes drift and gravity while a ≈1.5 s average removes the cardiac component, leaving the 0.1–0.6 Hz effort waveform on whichever axis carries the most respiratory-band variance. Rate is tracked spectrally, not counted from that waveform; the zero-crossing breath count is retained alongside as a cross-check.

Respiratory rateChest-ACC Rate, Spectral + Viterbi
Advanced

Breathing rate from the chest accelerometer: 60 s windows on a 30 s hop, band-passed 0.13–0.5 Hz, tracked as a spectral ridge with Viterbi smoothing (σ 1.2 br/min) and blended with a time-domain estimate. The tracker abstains below a confidence of 0.28, and a window under 80% coverage is not recording — it leaves the coverage denominator, because a sensor that was off is not a sensor that failed to produce a rate.

Method
ridge over 0.1–0.6 Hz (6–36 br/min) at 5 Hz analysis rate · Viterbi σ = 1.2 br/min time-domain blend weight 0.3 (per Charlton 2016) · abstains below conf 0.28
Validation (project corpus)Result
Corpus49 ACC/CPAP-paired nights · 8,057 epochs, H10 chest ACC vs ResMed CPAP flow
MAE1.10 br/min (95% CI 0.96–1.27), at 100% coverage
Within 2 br/min90.8% of epochs (97.2% at 70% coverage, MAE 0.63)
Null baselinePredicting the corpus median scores MAE 1.64 on the same epochs
Validated on a single subject — that is why the tier is emerging, not validated. Posture robustness is untested: the validation corpus spans essentially one posture, and no posture claim is made.
Effort amplitudeEffort Waveform RMS
Research

How strong the breathing movement is, as the RMS of the effort waveform — a surrogate for thoraco-abdominal effort, in g.

Formula
amplitude = RMS of the 0.1–0.6 Hz chest-ACC effort waveform
Surrogate on an uncalibrated scale (Ryser 2022) — no accuracy statement exists for the amplitude; compare within a wearer and a placement, never as an absolute.
Effort presentEffort Coverage Share
Research

The share of recorded 10 s epochs in which effort is detectable at all — a coverage statement, not an amplitude.

Formula
present iff epoch RMS ≥ 0.004 g; effortPresent = present epochs / recorded epochs epochs with < 3 samples read null and leave the denominator
The 0.004 g floor is experimental and uncalibrated (Ryser 2022 scale). Absence here means “no effort detected”, stated over recorded epochs only.

Signal Quality
Motion SQI → the confidence the Integrator sees

One confidence number summarises how much of the stream is trustworthy. It is computed per stream: when the actigraphy stream’s source differs from the position stream’s, actigraphy carries its own SQI. On export, a placeholder SQI (no data, or a unit that could not be determined) is published as null with its reason — while a measured 0 is a measurement and stays 0.

Signal qualityMotion SQI Confidence
Advanced

A 0–1 confidence from three direct detectors over the stream’s magnitude series.

Formula
conf = clamp( 1 − clipFrac·5 − flatFrac − offFrac·2, 0, 1 )
DetectorSample ruleFlag at
clip|mag| > 7.5 g (impact range)clipFrac > 2%
flatlinesuccessive Δmag < 1e-4flatFrac > 50%
sensor-off|mag| < 0.05 g (near-zero)offFrac > 20%
Fewer than 10 rows, or a unit that cannot be determined, returns conf 0 with a named flag (“no-data” / “unit-unknown”) instead of a quiet number. An undetermined ACC unit is never defaulted to milli-g: every magnitude-derived output refuses with that reason, while time-only facts are unaffected.

Known Limitations
The interpretation context for single-signal inertial analysis
ℹ️
These limitations are inherent to one accelerometer signal in an uncalibrated frame. They do not invalidate MotionDex outputs but define the appropriate interpretation context.
📍 Frame & Placement
  • The device frame is uncalibrated — posture names are a convention
  • The left/right sign has no grounded reference
  • Position prefers the chest stream; the wrist fallback reads a different body site
  • GYRO and MAGN streams are parsed and counted, not computed on
🧠 Interpretation
  • No sleep staging or apnea typing in this node — those are Integrator fusions
  • Respiratory rate is validated on one subject and one posture
  • Effort is a movement surrogate, not airflow or pressure
  • Actigraphy has no PSG validation in this project
📊 Algorithmic
  • Uncovered epochs are absence (null), never stillness or zero
  • A clock seam refuses the immobile fraction rather than estimating it
  • An undetermined ACC unit refuses every magnitude-derived output, by name
  • Sampling rates are measured, never assumed — an unmeasurable rate refuses the stream
⚖️ Regulatory
  • Not FDA cleared or CE marked as a medical device
  • Not for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions
  • Personal, research, and wellness use only
  • Discuss medical concerns with a qualified clinician

Intended use & safety

Tepna computes biometric patterns from your wearable and sensor data to support personal self-quantification. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure, screen for, or prevent any disease or condition, and is not a substitute for professional clinical evaluation. It has not been reviewed or cleared by the FDA, CE, or any regulatory body. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health. Use at your own risk. For research and personal use only. 100% local — no data leaves your device.
