A ring stall is judged by the witness the ring does carry, instead of counting as lost signal by default. The O2Ring's `_SPO2.csv` primary has no device column and its raw streams' `sensor timestamp [ns]` is drawn (`sample_index × 7,953,045 ns`, one delta value at 100.0 % across 16 files), so the delay test — which compares a host gap against a device-counter step — had nothing to read and every event-loop stall fell to `loss`. Note the wording carefully: the ring HAS a clock, an onboard RTC deliberately disciplined by `SET_UTC_TIME (0xC0)`; what its exported axis carries is no per-sample readings of it. `loss_audit.signal_witness` assembles `duration_s` from every `OXYFRAME.txt` of the night — a counter of seconds of signal PRODUCED, not seconds of time, which is precisely what makes it a witness here — and `witness_judges` reads it either side of a gap: advancing by the gap's length within one count means the ring produced those seconds and only the delivery was lost, not advancing means they never existed. A gap the counter cannot bracket, a night with no frame file, and a counter that went backwards because a new session reset it are all `unwitnessed`, reported under `unwitnessed_min` and deliberately NOT in `lost_min`, which feeds `worn_lost_min` and from there the verdict's `worn_but_not_recorded_fraction` — loss is a claim that the signal does not exist, and an unjudgeable gap is not that claim. Measured over the four corpus nights whose ring SpO2 actually stalls: claimed loss 4.5 min → 1.6 min of demonstrable silence, 2.3 min of delivery delay, 0.5 min unjudgeable (2026-09-20 0.2→0.1, 09-21 0.4→0.1, 09-22 1.0→0.2, 09-24 2.9→1.2). Scoped to the ring's own primary, not to "any stream without a counter": `duration_s` says nothing about an H10, so a Polar file that carries no device column keeps exactly the behaviour it had, and #3157's delay classification and #3163's fragment totals are unchanged. `gaps`, `by_cause` and `lost_min` still come from one list — `lost_min` subtracts the unwitnessed portion computed from that same list rather than filtering one of the three.
