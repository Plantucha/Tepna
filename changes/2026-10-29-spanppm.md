`DexClock.hostAxis` publishes `spanMs` and `spanPpm` beside its `ppm`, and PpgDex and ECGDex forward
them. `ppm` is an end-point ratio of the width-21 running median, so `spanPpm` gives the same ratio over
the raw divergence and the pair sizes what the smoother did. 173 committed PpgDex exports quoted a rate
with no span, which §7 forbids. Both fields are additive and no existing value moves. The treatment-response yield
pin re-stamps its closure hashes, and a paper-scale re-cut reproduced the yield exactly.
