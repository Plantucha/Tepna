A night's session span no longer depends on the zone of the box reading it. `nightqc.summarize` subtracted a FLOATING civil filename stamp from an ABSOLUTE `mtime`, so one real night measured 116,853 s in UTC, 102,453 s in America/New_York and 149,253 s in Asia/Tokyo — one offset apart each time, and 32.5 h for a single session in the UTC reading. One rule now governs every hop: each civil stamp the box writes is read as floating (the components as written), and the night's RECOVERED writer UTC offset is the single place a floating value becomes an instant; absent it, every span that needs one is UNKNOWN with a named reason rather than a guess. Six conversion sites moved together, not the three the residue row named — and two of the three newly found were live defects of their own, because `_midnight_of` and the cross-midnight contiguity probe both made POOLING move with the reader's zone, so the same night pooled in one zone and not another. The offset is recovered as the modal bucket of `mtime − floating(last row)` at the 15-minute quantum of the quantity (every UTC offset in use since 1972 is a whole multiple of it), with a ±14 h refusal bound checked before any vote is counted — measured need, since the suite's own fixtures write a constant host stamp that recovered a 67-day "offset" behind a clean majority — and a support floor of ≥2 unanimous voters or ≥3 with a strict majority, whose revision is recorded in three steps on the constant. Two decisions committed to earlier turned out to be wrong and are recorded as such: flooring the vote (the true value sits exactly ON a bucket boundary, so a one-millisecond shortfall moved a recovered zone fifteen minutes on the real 2026-09-27), and a floor whose own rationale — outvote a single outlier — did not bind the unanimous case it refused. Corpus control over 82 folders: 72 of 72 votable nights recover +14,400 s with no second value anywhere, outliers 0.81 % of 12,786 voters, and ten refusals each explained (7 non-night folders, a ring offline-sync tree with no host stamps at all, one two-voter disagreement, and one three-session night whose two killed sessions split the vote three ways). `summarize`/`build` also gain an optional `writer_offset` so a caller that KNOWS the frame can declare it — published as `basis: "declared"`, never blended with `"recovered"` — which is the seam a recorded offset will enter through when the writer records one. Fixes a crash found on the way: in the floating frame a file with no start stamp cannot be placed, so a night could hold rows and yield no session, and reading the judged session off that raised `TypeError` inside the QC poller, costing the night its entire summary.
