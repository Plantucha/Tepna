Badge the numbers three surfaces write into prose, and resolve the pattern-tab labels to their real registry grade.

A number inside a sentence escaped the badge mandate, because the mandate sits on the label and
prose carries no label. Three surfaces printed one with no grade at all: the HRVDex Distributions and
Patterns tab (the histogram, correlation and weekday stat lines), the OxyDex control stager's
inner-validation kappa, and the PulseDex context banner (elevation, the VO2max altitude factor, the
periodic-breathing index, the mode score). The registry held every HRVDex pattern metric and graded
it, but the tab spells a label with its unit inside, so no spelling resolved and the fallback would
have drawn an experimental disc over three validated metrics. This change teaches the registry those
seven spellings first, then badges the series. The three pattern labels the registry has no entry for
keep the experimental floor and the test group declares them, so a new pattern metric that resolves
to nothing reds a gate instead of taking the floor in silence.
