F12 claimed the Verity GYRO column is raw LSB (16.384× dps) across 543 corpus files, and prescribed a unit conversion in `ppgdex-dsp.js`. The finding carried its own instruction to reproduce before driving a DSP edit. Reproduced across every IMU file rather than one night: all 61 GYRO files declare `[dps]`, p50 4–37 and max 231–350, and NOT ONE file in the corpus has `max|gyro| > 2000` — the unmissable tell of an LSB column. All 113 ACC files declare `[mg]` and read 995 mg at rest; all 57 MAG files declare `[G]`. The claim does not reproduce. The repro that motivated it measured something else: dividing an already-dps column by 16.384 drives the gyro term to ≈0, and the brief's own no-gyro control (68% / 100.4 ms) is the same number as its "fixed" result (66% / 99.2 ms) — the conversion deleted the gyro rather than correcting it. Read forward, p99 of 174 dps against a `v/40` normaliser means ordinary arm movement saturates the gate, so the real defect is the THRESHOLD, not the unit; that is now the open item and needs a threshold justified against the measured distribution. No DSP change shipped — applying the conversion would have made a correct column wrong by 16×, with a green suite, because nothing gates the unit of an input.
