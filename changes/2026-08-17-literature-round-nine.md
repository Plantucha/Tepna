13h.1 CIRCULAR STATISTICS [REINVENTED, missing its null]. JOINT-UNWRAP §5 gates on "phase
concentration 0.15-0.38, where 1 is total agreement" and reads a rising trend by eye. That statistic
IS the mean resultant length of circular statistics - the per-block offsets wrapped modulo one RR are
angles on a circle of circumference one RR. What the field adds is the NULL the ad-hoc version lacked:
the Rayleigh test answers "at this n, is this concentration distinguishable from a uniform phase?" -
exactly the "is there a phase to regress" falsifier, with a p-value instead of an eyeballed threshold.
Berens 2009 (10.18637/jss.v031.i10) and Mardia & Jupp (10.1002/9780470316979 - Crossref dates it 1999
against the print convention's 2000, recorded so a citation matches the ledger, not the habit).

13h.2 SPIKE-TRAIN DISTANCE METRICS [MISSING, with a named consumer]. dead-ends §2.7's correction names
the outstanding measurement verbatim: a beat-correspondence audit, because matching beat counts
refutes net dropout but not local insertion/deletion pairs that preserve the total while scrambling
which foot belongs to which beat. That is, word for word, what an edit distance on point processes
measures. The Victor-Purpura distance (10.1152/jn.1996.76.2.1310): insert/delete cost 1, shift cost
q*|dt|, and 2/q is the timescale at which moving a beat costs as much as delete+insert - the "same
beat shifted, or a different beat?" boundary made explicit as a parameter. The DP that computes it
returns the ALIGNMENT itself - which beats pair, which are indels - i.e. the audit, not just a score.
van Rossum 2001 (10.1162/089976601300014321; Crossref family is "Rossum", alias owed if ledgered) is
the kernel form; Kreuz 2013 SPIKE-distance (10.1152/jn.00873.2012) is parameter-free.

This is also the form of §4's cycle-slip line that SURVIVES the objection it was closed under: the
GNSS detector was not owed because the unwrap was the wrong construction, and a VP alignment never
unwraps - it aligns. The concrete next measurement is stated: VP alignment between the ECG R-peak
train and the PPG foot train on the two §2-RESULT-IV nights where the anchor is identifiable, counting
insertion/deletion pairs directly. The diagnosis §3.4 warning transfers verbatim: association
quantifies ambiguity and must never force a coupling result.
