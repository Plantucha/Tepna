tools/pr-walker.mjs with tepna-pr-walker.{service,timer}: the landing pipeline runs itself. Every open PR is classified (held · conflict · red · pending · stuck · unknown · green) and the walker marks green drafts ready under the ≤ 4 cap oldest-first, re-runs a red required check once per head SHA, rebases a conflicting fleet branch with rebase-safe once per head SHA, and updates or merges a green PR only after Kodiak has been quiet 20 min. The board renders its landing page. Replaces the owner-typed undraft queue.
