`audits/CITATION-VERIFICATION-2026-08-05.json` recorded the truth for 86 DOIs and enforced nothing. A Node-lane gate now asserts that for every DOI on a reader-facing source surface, the surrounding citation names the ledger's first author and a year within ±1 — 167 occurrences across 10 surfaces. Built before the defects were fixed, it independently rediscovered exactly the four §1/§2 defects and nothing else, which is the evidence that it works. `integrator-dsp.js` credited 10.3390/s21144777 to Straczkiewicz (actually **Brønd JC, Pedersen NH, Larsen KT, Grøntved A**) and 10.3390/jimaging8050120 to Bent B (actually **Xiao R, Ding C, Hu X**) — titles and DOIs correct, so the link resolves and lands on the paper being described, which is the failure a reader cannot detect; the same wrong attribution was repeated in a `tests/dex-tests.js` comment and is fixed there too. `ecgdex-dsp.js`/`ppgdex-dsp.js` cited 10.1088/1361-6501/ae6a09 as a bare DOI, now attributed to **Abdessalem K. (2026), Meas Sci Technol** per `CLAUDE.md` §📚. Three noise sources are handled, each of which would otherwise bury a real finding: corporate authors get `authorAliases` (the ledger had none, and Crossref's last-word parse of the ESC/NASPE Task Force falsely redded three correct guides), ±1 year is agreement (Crossref `issued` is online-first), and author matching is whole-word with no length floor — a `>= 4` filter silently dropped every three-letter surname and scored "Pan & Tompkins 1985" as a mismatch. DOI *existence* is deliberately not gated: that needs network, and it is the weak half — all three wrong-paper DOIs resolved perfectly. The gate was verified by making it fail twice (a swapped ledger author, a year shifted outside the band) before being trusted.
