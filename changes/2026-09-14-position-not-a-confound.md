`papers/odi4-ahi-bias.html` §3.2 Table 6: body position is **not** a confound of the ODI-4 agreement,
measured rather than argued.

The scored desaturation rate does vary strongly by position code (14.6–20.0 events/h, a 37 % spread).
The agreement does not follow it. Nights spending ≥50 % in the highest-rate code show a raw residual
of −0.51 against −0.16 — but they are also sicker (expert index 4.03 vs 2.96), and within
expert-index strata the direction reverses: supine-dominant is worse at 5–15 and better at 15–30.

An effect that changes sign between adjacent strata is not an effect. Stated with its power: n=300,
and the top strata carry 8 and 17 nights, so this excludes a large consistent effect, not a small one.
