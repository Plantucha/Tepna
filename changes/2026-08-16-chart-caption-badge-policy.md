Owner decision on `DEEP-AUDIT-V-FOLLOWUPS` §1.2: a chart caption is not a badge site — badge the
series instead, option (c).

A caption routinely spans two metrics — "SpO₂ Mean % · T95% Time Below 95%" — so it cannot carry one
evidence tier, and forcing one onto it would be exactly the fabricated authority §🎫 exists to prevent,
one level up from the defect the gate was built to catch. `CLAUDE.md` §🎫 already mandated a badge on
"every chart-or-graph series"; the corollary is now stated explicitly so it is not re-litigated: an
unbadged caption is correct provided every series it draws is badged.

`no-fabricated-tier` therefore no longer scans `chartTitle`, and the ratchet drops from 94 to 70.

The 24 were measured rather than taken from the brief's arithmetic. Instrumenting the gate to attribute
each offender to the helper that produced it gave row 2, chartTitle 24, metric 55, ssKPI 7, nrChip 6 —
94 in total — so removing captions lands exactly on 70. Both ratchet assertions pass at that value, not
just the does-not-grow one: the not-slack assertion requires the debt to sit within five of the cap, so
a wrong number would have reddened rather than passed quietly.

The remaining 70 are the genuine per-metric debt — 68 metric tiles plus two `row` labels — and stay
ratcheted pending adjudication. Those were never auto-fixable and are not touched here.
