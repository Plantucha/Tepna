The PpgDex app now corrects its sample rate against the host clock. `PpgDex.src.html` inlines `clock.js`, so the `typeof DexClock` guards in `ppgdex-dsp.js` reach `DexClock.hostAxis` in the browser. Before this change the app refused the axis with `no clock spine in this bundle` and kept the device rate, while the fold and the test suite loaded `clock.js` and corrected it. The app and the corpus therefore disagreed on every Verity and ring night with host stamps.

The node keeps its own timestamp parser, and no parser delegates to `DexClock`. `CLAIM clockBundles` moves from 5 to 6. `tools/realm-manifest.js` adds `clock.js` to `PPG_REALM`, so the regenerator and the equivalence gate run the realm the app ships. The PpgDex fixtures carry the regenerated outputs.
