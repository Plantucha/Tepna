The PpgDex app now corrects its sample rate against the host clock. `PpgDex.src.html` inlines `clock.js`, so the `typeof DexClock` guards in `ppgdex-dsp.js` reach `DexClock.hostAxis` in the browser. Before this change the app refused the axis with `no clock spine in this bundle` and kept the device rate. The fold and the test suite loaded `clock.js` and corrected the rate. So the app and the corpus disagreed on every Verity night and every host-disciplined ring night. An unstamped finger night stays on the 125 Hz crystal by design (`ppgdex-dsp.js:1446`).

The node keeps its own timestamp parser, and no parser delegates to `DexClock`. `CLAIM clockBundles` moves from 5 to 6. `tools/realm-manifest.js` adds `clock.js` to `PPG_REALM`, so the regenerator and the equivalence gate run the realm the app ships. The PpgDex fixtures carry the regenerated outputs.
