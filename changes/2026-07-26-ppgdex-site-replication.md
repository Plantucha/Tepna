Decide the PPG optical site on channel replication rather than column count. The O2Ring emits both a 1-column pleth and a 3-column file whose three columns are the same reading replicated, so column-count classification tagged the ring as a Verity armband on every 3-column night — finger morphology was graded on the wrist's evidence tier and the 156-sentinel pass was skipped on 526 files in the 2026-07 corpus alone. Measured over that corpus, O2Ring three-column files have 100.0% of rows identical across channels and Verity files 0.0%, min equal to max in both: perfect separation. Re-running the five audited nights, every one now reads finger and the recovered sentinel handling lifts analyzable time (07-20: 99% to 100%, correction rate 2.3% to 0.7%).
