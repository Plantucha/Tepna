Adds `--scan` to `tools/beat-capture-recapture.mjs` and records the result, which closes this line for the current corpus. Windows are ranked by DISAGREEMENT COUNT — a property of the three beat sets computed before any estimate exists, i.e. selection on precision and never on the estimate, the miss rate, or whether the estimator returned ok. Over the same 18.8-minute night: 9 windows, **median disagreement per window = 0**, and **0 of 9 identifiable**. In seven of nine windows the three detectors agree on EVERY beat; all disagreement is confined to two windows (26 and 22 beats) and even there the informative cells stay single-digit. **The limit this exposes is structural, not a shortage of data:** perfect agreement has two causes that capture-recapture cannot separate — nothing was missed, or all three detectors missed the same beats — and the second is exactly the positive-dependence case the method was adopted to handle. In the limit of total dependence the overlap carries no information, which the tool's own self-test already pins. Quiet sleep does not merely lack power; the question is unidentifiable there by construction. The honest next method is a different one: astronomy's artificial-star test and gravitational-wave injection campaigns face the same no-ground-truth problem and solve it the other way round — plant synthetic events of known amplitude into the real recording and measure what the shipped detector recovers, giving completeness as a function of SNR. That needs no second detector, no independence assumption and no disagreement, and it measures the quantity `beat-error-recovery.mjs` currently has to assume, since that tool perturbs an existing beat train rather than the waveform the detector actually reads. Recorded as the recommendation rather than built; capture-recapture remains correct for artefact-heavy segments where detectors genuinely diverge.
