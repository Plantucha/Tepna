Ask a spare radio an HCI round trip before failing over onto it, so a controller that reports `UP RUNNING` while answering nothing can no longer cost every wearable a disconnect, a re-bond and a hole in the recording; a spare that does not answer is quarantined for a cooldown, and a box whose every spare is deaf refuses the migration instead of spending its flap cap.
