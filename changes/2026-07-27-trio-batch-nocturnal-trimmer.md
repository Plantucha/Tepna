`tools/trio-batch.mjs`'s majority-nocturnal gate is now a **trimmer**, not an admission test. It ran per WINDOW, so it could only admit or reject — and a window is a cluster of blocks, so a cluster that is 61 % nocturnal passed *carrying its daytime block inside it*. On 2026-07-26 an awake 14:31→20:01 block sat 1.7 h from the 21:40→05:10 sleep, under `SLEEP_GAP_H = 4`, so the two never split into separate clusters; the merged window scored 61 % and was admitted whole. Same shape on 2026-07-17 (72 %) and 2026-07-16 (89 %). This is the contamination the gate was added to prevent, entering through the one door the whole-window form leaves open.

New `nocturnalBlocks()` applies the SAME `> 0.5` fraction PER BLOCK, so a cluster contributes exactly its nocturnal blocks. Still a fraction, never an absolute clip — a 19:00→02:00 sleep is 71 % nocturnal and survives WHOLE; blocks are dropped, never cut at the band edge, because sleep onset is not 21:00. `--keep-daytime` bypasses it as before. What the trim dropped is printed block by block (CLAUDE.md: no silent caps), and a night that then fails `--min-overlap` says so *because of the trim* rather than just reporting a small number.

Tool-only — no bundle, no `manifestHash`, no fixture. Measured over the real 2026-07-16…26 capture corpus: **all 11 nights still fold** (none lost), and every one is now **99–100 % nocturnal** (was 61–100 %). 2026-07-26 goes 11.8 h/61 % → 7.2 h/100 %, and folds to 33 desat · 36 surge · 0 matched · 7.3 h — matching a control fold built from that night's sleep files alone (33 · 36 · 0 · 7.1 h), which is the independent check that the trim recovers the uncontaminated night. Corpus median culprit σ 4.70 → 3.23 bpm; per-node medians move in BOTH directions (PpgDex 4.70→3.23, ECGDex 0.99→1.91, OxyDex 1.23→1.72) and at 7 estimated nights of 11 that is not yet a σ improvement claim — the verified claim is that the daytime is out. `--selftest` extended with the 2026-07-26 cluster to scale (whole-window would admit it; the trimmer keeps one block, the sleep one), the straddling-sleep control, an all-daytime cluster, and the exactly-half boundary: **all green**.
