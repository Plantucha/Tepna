`--blind-prepare` / `--blind-score` turn "hand the analysis to another operator" from an intention into two commands. This work preregistered its criteria and still withdrew two claims, both caught by the same operator running one more check — the mechanism that cannot be relied on — and an instruction nobody follows is not a control. Prepare writes `blinded.json` (anchor pairs and opaque ids) plus a sealed `TRUTH.json`; the analyst reports `{id -> recoveredPpm}` from the blinded file alone; score compares. **What is hidden is stated plainly**: device, night, whether a perturbation was applied at all, its family and its magnitude — including whether the draw is a NULL. The menu is deliberately published (an analyst can read the source); the secret is the DRAW, and the key is caller-supplied so the tool holds no hidden state and a disputed result can be re-derived rather than re-argued. Audited on the real 35.7 MB artefact: zero matches for device names, nights, draw ids or magnitude fields. **Two harness defects were found by running it rather than reasoning about it, and both are recorded in the code.** (1) The truth was stored as a DELTA while the analyst can only report an ABSOLUTE rate — they never receive a baseline to subtract — which scored 21 of 61 streams as false positives when every one was the estimator working correctly; the baseline is now computed at prepare time, where the unperturbed anchors are still in hand. (2) A frequency draw cannot be applied to a zero-span stream, so expecting a recovery there demands the impossible: `freq-pos-mid` scored a median error of exactly 100.000 ppm — the injected magnitude — because 10 draws landed on the corpus's zero-span drawn streams. Those are now marked inapplicable and reported as a count, never silently dropped. End-to-end on the corpus: 51 answered, 10 inapplicable, median |err| 0.000 ppm, worst 1.025, zero false positives on null draws, reproducing the open-label offset and packet-loss findings blind. Both legs were run by the same operator, so this is NOT itself a blind result — it proves the mechanism and removes the excuse.
