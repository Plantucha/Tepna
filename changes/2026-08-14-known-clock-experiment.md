Records the owner's known-clock adversarial capture design, and the four measured facts that change it. The framing the brief leads with: every timing number the suite publishes is **self-consistent but unanchored** — `hostAxis` measures device against host, Allan measures host against itself, closure measures three sources against each other, and nothing has ever been compared to a truth known in advance — so this is the only experiment in the backlog that can fail in a way the others structurally cannot. **[WITHDRAWN 2026-08-15 — see `2026-08-15-stratum-claim-withdrawn.md`: the box is source-stratum 1 and PASSES the gate; `chronyc`'s tracking-stratum is normalised by −1 before the comparison.]** This changeset originally stated that the box is Stratum 2 and therefore fails `host_clock.py`'s own `TIMEBASE_MAX_STRATUM = 1` rate-trust gate. What survives is that its 19.5 µs RMS offset is ~10⁴ tighter than the BLE jitter that immediately follows, so PPS-disciplining buys precision the transport destroys; the **O2Ring cannot be a third clock leg** because its axis is drawn, which makes it the experiment's negative control (every recovery target must refuse on it); **constant offset is not identifiable from beat matching** (alignment holds only mod one RR) so its criterion must rest on an aperiodic marker; and the box has **2 BLE adapters for 3 devices**, confounding any sensor-specific noise term with adapter assignment. Adds a preregistration protocol (`acceptance.json` committed before the first blind run) and a mandatory pre-committed null night, since a pipeline that recovers a perturbation from an unperturbed night has failed and nothing else in the design catches it.
