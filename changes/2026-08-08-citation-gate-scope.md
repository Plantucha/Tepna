§2 asked whether `docs/` and `briefs/` belong in the citation gate's scope, since silence about a surface reads as coverage. Answered by measuring each — and the section asked about the wrong two surfaces: it missed **`papers/`**, where three published papers carry **32 DOIs**, authored and reader-facing and entirely ungated. That is the surface where a wrong author list does the most damage, because the link still resolves and still lands on the paper being described. `papers/**` and `docs/**.md` are now IN (32 and 4 occurrences, **zero** problems each — gating a clean surface is the point: correct today, and now it cannot drift). `briefs/` is OUT on evidence rather than a hunch: 49 occurrences, **17 problems, all false**, because a brief quotes a wrong attribution in order to say it is wrong — the top offender being `CITATION-ATTRIBUTION-FOLLOWUPS` itself, tripping four times on the defects it fixed. `docs/*.html` stays out as served copies already gated at their source. Every verdict and its reason now lives in the gate's own comment, which is what §2 asked for. Scope is pinned **structurally** by three assertions naming the surfaces, not by a count floor — a count cannot distinguish "papers/ was dropped" from "a paper lost a citation", and dropping a surface is the failure that passes quietly because a narrower gate is always greener. Verified by making it fail on a newly covered file: corrupting one surname in `papers/acc-respiratory-rate.html` reds 4 DOIs. The first falsifier attempt replaced only the first of six occurrences, left every citation window intact and passed — recorded, because a falsifier that does not reach the code reports the gate as fine. 167 → 203 occurrences, 10 → 14 surfaces.
