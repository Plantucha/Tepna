Adds §7 to the cross-domain follow-ups: epidemiology's capture-recapture, imported to answer the largest open finding from `KNOWN-CLOCK-ADVERSARIAL-CAPTURE` — **how many beats did every detector miss?** That question gates the whole suite, because one missed beat in a thousand inflates rMSSD by 20.8 % against ~0.003 % for the entire clock-error family, and it is also the only route to deciding whether Malik correction's −22 % on a clean train is bias or correct repair. Current practice in the field (the PPG-beats toolbox) establishes reference beats as those TWO ECG detectors both found — an intersection, which by construction discards the beats both missed, i.e. exactly the population being sought; sound as a quality filter, biased as truth. Lincoln–Petersen estimates that unobserved class from the overlap. **The decisive constraint, checked rather than assumed:** the estimator requires independent capture, our detectors fail together (motion, perfusion, apnea degrade optical and electrical at once), and positive dependence biases the estimate DOWNWARD — it would under-report the very undercount it is measuring. With two lists the model is saturated so the dependence cannot be estimated at all; the remedy is a log-linear model with interaction terms, needing ≥3 sources. We have three genuinely different instruments (H10 electrical/chest, Verity optical/arm, O2Ring optical/finger). **Measured 2026-08-15 and it eliminates a fourth candidate:** the Verity firmware `_RR.txt` is EMPTY — 0 beats over the same night the H10 firmware reports 15,522 — confirming CLAUDE.md's documented all-zero onboard files on this night, so a Verity beat must come from raw PPG; and H10-firmware vs H10-Pan–Tompkins are not independent, being the same waveform, so they cannot be two of the three. Done-when requires the interaction terms to be REPORTED, since a fit finding no dependence on this data would be the surprising result and must not pass silently.
