PpgDex now flags the HRV failure its confidence gate was structurally unable to see. **Six of 37 corpus nights published whole-record rMSSD of 91–188 ms — against a chest ECG reading 26–42 ms on the same night, same window — every one of them stamped `lowConfidence: false`.**

**The defect.** `hrvLowConfidence` was `analyzablePct < 60 || correctionRate > 20`: a pure **coverage** test. On all six nights every coverage field was healthy — analyzable 96–100 %, correction 2.5–13 %, LED agreement 96–100, motion-rejected ≤ 1.7 — so no threshold on coverage could reach them without also rejecting good nights. The contamination is not missing data, it is a **shape**: an alternating short/long interval sequence. Ruled out in turn — not beat doubling (mean HR agrees with ECG to ≤ 0.5 bpm), not session concatenation (the elevation is in *every* 5-min epoch; 2026-06-29 median epoch rMSSD 193.3, min 44.4), and epoch `lf/hf` collapses to 0.1–0.6 against 0.4–5.4 on a clean night — spectral energy piled at the RR-series Nyquist, most likely intermittent dicrotic-notch locking in the foot detector.

**The detector is free and needs no new statistic.** Over a whole night, successive-difference dispersion cannot exceed overall dispersion: rMSSD measures beat-to-beat change, `sdnnRobust` the spread those beats are drawn from, so `rmssd > sdnnRobust` is not a physiological state — it is an artifact signature. It holds on **all six and none of the other 31**, and the two next-most-divergent nights (2026-07-01, 61.7 vs 64.1; 07-02, 52.0 vs 57.9) sit just *under* the line, so the ordering is real rather than a coincidence of six. Compared against `sdnnRobust` rather than `sdnn` deliberately: whole-record `sdnn` runs high on optical through SDANN/baseline-wander inflation, which would mask the very violation being looked for.

`PPGDSP.hrvShapeViolates(rmssdMs, sdnnRobustMs)` is exported as a pure predicate (testable without driving `analyze`), returns **false** when either input is absent — a record too short for the robust median gets no verdict, because an absent comparand is not evidence of good shape — and the flag joins the existing coverage disjunct. The reason string names the shape rather than coverage, and `hrvShapeViolation` is surfaced as its own field (`hrv.time.shapeViolation`, present only when true, so no clean night's export moves): coverage and shape warrant different consumer responses — a sparse night can still be down-weighted and pooled, an alternating one must be discarded.

**Downstream, this is not cosmetic.** The Integrator already excludes any PpgDex leg self-reporting `lowConfidence` from the HRV consensus (`integrator-dsp` FU §2), so those six nights stop feeding a 3–6× inflated rMSSD into fusion. It is also why PpgDex is the TCH-named culprit on 22 of 37 nights (median σ: ECGDex 0.91, OxyDex 1.09, **PpgDex 2.71 bpm**).

**Verified on the corpus.** Re-folding the six affected nights plus three controls: all six now carry `lowConfidence: true` + `shapeViolation: true`, the three controls (07-01, 07-02, 07-27) are untouched, and **every rMSSD value is byte-identical** — the change flags, it does not alter a number.

**Coverage.** New group `PpgDex HRV shape gate` (19 assertions) pins the six real nights as flagged, five clean ones — including both nearest misses — as untouched, the absent/non-finite/equality edges, and then *measures* rather than restates the premise: a synthetic alternating series run through the **shipped** `timeDomain()` really does put rMSSD above its own SDNN (800.0 vs 400.8) while a slow respiratory-sinus oscillation does not (3.0 vs 56.7). The first draft of that control was a `% 8` sawtooth, which trips the predicate for the right reason (it *is* a jumpy series) and would have proved nothing — the group caught its own harness.
