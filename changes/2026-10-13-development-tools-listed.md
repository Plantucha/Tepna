`THIRD-PARTY.md` gains a "Development tools (not shipped)" section: nineteen
tools with a version or pin, a licence, a use and the machines each runs on.
Every licence was read from the installed copy, its own licence file or the
tool's own output. Two rows read **not verified**, because no copy of those two
was available to read. The section states that the one GPL-licensed tool runs as
a separate program, and that nothing on the page reaches a bundle. `README.md`
points its acknowledgements at the new section.
