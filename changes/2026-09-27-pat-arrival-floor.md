PAT Feasibility's corrected lag is now re-timed on each device's **packet-arrival floor** instead of the old phone-stamp "ACC-sync". The old stage aligned the two accelerometers on phone arrival stamps, so its offset was the two Bluetooth links' buffering difference, and applying it made drift worse (09-25: 151 → 422 ms, verdict WINDOW-CENSORED). The worker now anchors each device's counter on its sharpest arrival sidecar stream. The floor is the 1st percentile of (arrival − the packet's LAST sample) per 10-min window. The last sample is used because the first carries the packet-fill time and smeared the Verity edge (118 → 11 ms spread). A window is refused under 100 packets or at a spread > 10 ms, and a > 30 s jump is a counter step. R-peaks and feet are re-timed as device time plus floor, and the corrected coupling is re-gated as `vdCorr` (`corrected(floor)` in the verdict cell). The accelerometers become a motion check that should read ≈ 0 within its stated ±25 ms resolution. A night without sidecars refuses with the reason; there is no phone-stamp fallback. The raw legs and the primary verdict are byte-identical. On 09-25 and 09-26 the correction removes 158 and 156 ms of buffering difference and cuts drift 151 → 87 and 186 → 69 ms (FEASIBLE on both). The motion check reads +57 and −31 ms, outside its resolution, so an inter-device uncertainty of tens of ms remains and the page shows it. The night index lists both Polar devices' sidecars as `arrival`, and the monitor's PAT click hands them over.
