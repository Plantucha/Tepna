Paper Draft v4 — the beat detector itself, which the paper had measured the CONSEQUENCES of (§3.8: one missed beat in a thousand costs 20.8 % of rMSSD) without ever measuring the rate. Adds §3.11, two no-ground-truth methods imported from other fields. **Capture-recapture (epidemiology) does not work here and the reason is the finding:** three independent detectors on one recording agreed on 935 of 986 beats, the informative cells collapsed to single digits, and the closed form returned 41 % of the recording as unseen; it now refuses, reporting that the data cannot identify the undercount and explicitly not that nothing was missed. Ranking windows by disagreement did not rescue it — in seven of nine windows all three detectors agreed on every beat, and perfect agreement is equally consistent with nothing missed and with all three missing the same beats, so the quantity is unidentifiable by construction on quiet recordings. **The artificial-star test (observational astronomy) does work:** planting the subject's own averaged beat into the raw waveform at admissible positions gives a completeness curve with a sharp threshold at 20-30x local noise and zero induced false positives, placing ~1 % of real beats at risk. Adds §3.12, the correction to that: attenuating real beats IN SITU shows gap-planting OVERSTATES the detector by ~3x (1.56 % vs 0.53 % on a common axis) — the opposite sign to the caveat we published — driven by the adaptive threshold rather than searchback, isolated by attenuating a beat together with its neighbours (recovery 1.2 % -> ~74 %) and with the morphology alternative excluded by a second construction agreeing to ~11 %. Records the measurement artefact found in the process: template subtraction leaves a beat-shaped, beat-POSITIONED residual, so the nominal amplitude axis reported silence where the detector still saw signal, identified because detections at nominal zero sat a median 15.4 ms from the true beat rather than at the ~507 ms interval midpoint an inference would produce. §3.12's experiment was performed independently by another session and is reported with its numbers unchanged and credited to `SEARCHBACK-AWARE-INJECTION-2026-08-15-BRIEF.md`. Abstract, limitations, conclusion, reproduction block and references updated; Chao 1987 and the artificial-star design added as sources.
