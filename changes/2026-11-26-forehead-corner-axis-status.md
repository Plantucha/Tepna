Say the forehead corner's clock axis out loud on the four-corner night page. The corner's per-second
grid rides `relSec`, which takes the host-axis correction, and no surface stated whether that
correction ran, so a reader could not tell a corrected forehead map from a drawn one. The worker now
carries `ok`, `applied`, `drawn`, `deviceDrawn`, `independent`, `timingSource`, `anchors`, the drawn
column's own rate and the differential start correction, and the page prints them under one badge. A
drawn device column publishes no rate by design, so the page reports that figure and marks it as a
number no consumer may spend. A null reads as "not read", never as a no and never as a 0.
