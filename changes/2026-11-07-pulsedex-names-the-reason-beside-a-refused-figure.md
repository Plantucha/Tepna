PulseDex refuses its whole-record HRV figures on a recorded clock seam and writes the reason into the record as `hrvReason`. No render surface read that key.

The KPI strip prints an em-dash for every absent figure, so a clock seam, a short reading and an absent input all drew one glyph. A reader could see that rMSSD was absent and could not learn why.

The context banner now carries the reason the node itself decided. A refusal names its token beside the figures it explains. A sidecar the box wrote and this reading could not place states UNKNOWN, under the ruling that an unexamined channel is not a clean one.

A stream with no sidecar at all stays out of the banner. That token fits nearly every hand-dropped file, and a line on every reading teaches a reader to skip it. A measured zero stays silent for the same reason. The export states both for a consumer that needs them.
