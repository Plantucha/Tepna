`tools/nsrr-effort-typing.mjs` — measures, on SHHS1 RIP belts, the figure `integrator-dsp.js` and
`papers/effort-typing-null.html` currently CITE: that a single effort belt recovers 84 % of central
apneas (Nassi 2022), against our measured 16.5 % on a chest accelerometer.

Result at 150 records (1144 central, 6331 obstructive): **80.2 % of central apneas fall below half
their local baseline**, against a pre-stated ≥70 % band. Nassi transfers.

⚠️ The obstructive control reads 51.6 %, which is NOT far below the central figure — a single
thoracic belt under-reads obstructive effort because thorax and abdomen move out of phase, which the
Integrator brief already names as the thoracoabdominal paradox. So the separation is real but
narrower than the 16.5-vs-84 framing implies.
