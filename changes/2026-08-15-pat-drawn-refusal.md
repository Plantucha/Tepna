Continues the consumer migration off `hostAxis.independent`. `tools/pat-host-offset.mjs` reads the inter-device offset from each device's {devMs, hostMs} pair and gated only on `independent` — which compares two COLUMNS and therefore reads MORE independent the coarser a synthesised counter's quantisation. Its PAT target is the Wellue finger PPG (line 371), and that is exactly the drawn-axis device: **O2Ring streams are flagged drawn 20/20** in the corpus, against **0/6 for H10 ecg** and **0/19 for Verity ppg**. Without the guard `toHostAxis` places beats on a fabricated timebase and the offset it yields is an artefact of the assumed rate. Now refuses on `deviceDrawn`, naming DRAWN in the refusal so a reader can tell it from an independence refusal. **ECGDex is deliberately NOT gated**: its exposure is zero measured (H10 ecg 0/6), and adding the guard there would force re-cutting a legitimate planted-drift fixture to defend against something that does not occur in the data — machinery for a hypothetical. Both refusals live in CLI loops rather than exported surfaces, so they are gated as source text; `tools/pat-host-offset.mjs` is now wired into BOTH scan lanes (the node `wanted` list and `Dex-Test-Suite.html` SOURCE_FILES), since a file in only one lane makes the scan read nothing in the other. **The new gate was vacuous on its first version and that is recorded rather than quietly fixed**: matching `/deviceDrawn === true/` also matched the refusal BODY (`ax.deviceDrawn === true ? …`), so replacing the whole guard with `if (false)` left it green. The regex now pins the conditional, verified by mutation — clean 4/4, mutant 2 failing.
