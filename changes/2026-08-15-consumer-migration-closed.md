Closes the drawn-axis consumer migration, and **two of the four consumers were closed by declining to change them**. The flag was wired exactly where a drawn axis can arrive, and the corpus says where that is: O2Ring 20/20 drawn and Verity `ppi` 1/1, against H10 ecg **0/6** and Verity ppg **0/19**. `integrator-dsp.js` (#1274) had a measured specimen — one real O2Ring segment reporting −22.83 ppm at a 99.3 % drawn share, passing both existing guards. `tools/pat-host-offset.mjs` (#1278) takes the Wellue finger PPG as its PAT target, which is the drawn device itself. `ecgdex-dsp.js` is **deliberately not gated**: zero measured exposure, and adding the guard would force re-cutting a legitimate planted-drift fixture whose uniform device axis is by construction indistinguishable from a fabricated one. `pat-gate.js` is **deliberately not gated**: nothing live passes it an axis — `PAT Feasibility.html` calls neither `verdict(` nor `hostAxis(`. Both would have been machinery for a hypothetical, and adding them for symmetry would make the guard look thorough while teaching a reader the flag matters where it does not; the brief records that either decision reopens the moment its premise changes. Also records what the adapter-assignment item actually requires: `capture.py` holds a single global `ADAPTER` for bonding, not a per-device assignment, so BlueZ selects the controller and "record which radio carried which link" is a design change (the BlueZ object path carries it at connect time) plus hardware validation on a nightly-capturing box — not a log line, and not to be made blind.
