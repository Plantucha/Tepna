FOLLOWUPS §1.9 — `detectBreaths().breathRate` divided breaths by the WHOLE RECORDING while every
sibling ventilation figure computed beside it (`rrMaskOn`, `tvMaskOn`, `mvMaskOn`, `snMaskOn`,
`flMaskOn`) is mask-on filtered, so a surfaced breaths/min was diluted by mask-off time by a
different factor every night. Found by trying to USE it as §1.5's grade reference and rejecting it
on that ground.

- **Denominator is mask-on seconds**, numerator gated to mask-on too (breaths "detected" while the
  mask is off are noise crossings; counting them against a smaller denominator would over-correct).
- **The window is surfaced** as `breathMaskOnSec` beside the rate — CLAUDE.md §7's ppm rule
  generalised: never quote a rate without the window it was measured over.
- **No pressure lane ⇒ refuse with a reason**, not divide by the recording length.

⚠️ **Half-wired twice in one unit, which is the part worth reading.** `breathRate` is computed at
TWO sites — per-session and the night-level pool — both dividing by wall duration, and the export
reads the NIGHT one, so fixing the per-session site alone changed nothing observable. The night
aggregator then reads `s._pool`, a lightweight object SEPARATE from `metrics`, so the new
denominator had to travel there as well; until it did the twin returned `null` with `maskOnSec 0`.
Enumerating every reader of `breathRate` (dsp ×2 · registry · render tile · fusion row) is what
ended it.

**Committed twin** `cpapdex_maskoff_twin_{BRP,PLD}.edf` (`tools/gen-maskoff-twin.mjs`): 20 min
recorded, mask on for the first 10, ~150 breaths in the on-half. Pre-fix reports **7.5** br/min,
fixed **15.0**; mutation-checked — restoring the wall denominator reds 4 of the group's 5
assertions.

**The real corpus is silent about this, and that is measured rather than claimed:** mask-on was
1.000 on all 24 nights §1.5 folded, and across every committed CPAP golden the fix moves exactly ONE
rate — 16.8 → 16.9 on the synthetic EDF golden, a rounding shift on a ~full-mask-on record —
everything else purely additive (`breathMaskOnSec`). 5 goldens regenerated by
`tools/regen-cpap-goldens.mjs`, never hand-edited.

survives, because `verifiedUnder` is a property of the merged code (measured on #2085).
