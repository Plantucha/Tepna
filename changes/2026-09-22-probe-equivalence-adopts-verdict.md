`tools/probe-equivalence.mjs` emits one `tepna.verdict/1`, keyed on its CONTROLS like its `probe-clock-equivalence` sibling: the survivors are the population it adjudicates, so scoring one would convict the `if (lo < 0) lo = 0` → `<=` case its own header names as correct code. The criterion is RULE 1 made machine-readable — every declared family's battery must separate the same-function mutants a test already killed — so `FAIL` means a family is BLIND and its verdicts are void, convicting the battery rather than the subject file. Population is in families (checked = controls ran, clean or blind; excluded = withheld, counted by named reason). Adoption also named the PARTIAL control-load drop: `if (r.err) continue` skipped a control that failed to load before `ctlRan` counted it, and the only guard was the all-or-nothing `!ctlRan`, so 12 sampled with 8 dead printed `4/4 separated`. Its row moves from a copy-pasted `word-only` exemption to `decides`/`adopted`.
