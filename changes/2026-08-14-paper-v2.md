Closes targets 7 and 8 of the known-clock experiment and completes the paper as Draft v2, retitled because the evidence moved the thesis. **Target 8 — host-induced artifacts, measured rather than argued.** §2.1 had reasoned from a ratio that the host could not be the limiting term; that is an argument, not a measurement. A host disturbance is COMMON-MODE across two devices captured simultaneously by one host, so the two detrended residuals were correlated on a shared grid: median **r = 0.036** over 4 pairs (median 4.8 h overlap), i.e. ~3.6 % of residual variance is common-mode and the rest is device or transport. Residual SD is 42.6 ms (H10) / 48.9 ms (Verity) against a 19.5 us host RMS offset — **0.046 %** of what it would have to explain. **Target 7 — sensor-specific noise, with the adapter confound escaped.** The brief had recorded this target as blocked because device noise is confounded with which radio carried it. Comparing two streams from the SAME device on the SAME adapter on the SAME night at matched span removes that: at 4800 s the Verity accelerometer reads **322.8 ppm** sigma_y against the same device's optical stream at **42.0** — **7.7x**, and by construction not an adapter term. Its Allan slope is also the least steep (−0.971 vs −0.990), so it averages down slightly less well. All streams remain phase-noise dominated. **Paper v2** adds §3.8 (beat errors), §3.9 (host common-mode), §3.10 (stream-specific noise), rewrites the abstract, limitations and conclusion, and is retitled from "The estimator is fine; the quantity is not" to "We were measuring the wrong error" — because the beat substrate showed a 20.8 % rMSSD cost for a 1-in-1000 missed beat against ~0.003 % for the entire clock-error family, and a paper whose headline understates its own result is mis-titled. Limitations now state every small-n sub-analysis explicitly (9–11 streams for the span test, 4 pairs for host common-mode, 4–6 per row for stream noise) and note that the same operator injected and analysed.
