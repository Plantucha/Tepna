The trio fold now reads the validity sidecars the box writes. `tools/trio-batch.mjs` hands `_ECGRUNS.txt` to the H10 ECG parse and `_PPGRUNS.txt` to the Verity and the ring PPG parses. Before this change a blanking run the box recorded reached ECGDex and PpgDex as signal.

Both fold merges now keep the spans of every fragment. `mergePpg` dropped `rec.spans`, so the pinned-interval exclusion in PpgDex ran on no multi-fragment night, with or without a sidecar. The rebase rule lives in `tools/trio-fold-validity.mjs`, and `tests/trio-fold-validity-tests.mjs` plants it.

`parseECGLines` accepts `runsText`, and `parseECGText` delegates to it. A call without the option returns the same record as before. The ACC sidecars stay out of the fold, because the padded ACC union needs its own span rebase.
