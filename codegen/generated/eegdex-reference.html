EEGDex — Technical Reference

Metrics, Formulas &
Normal Values

Band-power and sleep-architecture analysis from consumer EEG (Muse). Direct spectral readouts, EEG-staged hypnogram, and derived micro-architecture markers.

27 metrics total: 13 Core · 11 Advanced · 3 Research

⚠️
Important: EEGDex is a scaffold, not a shipped node — as of 2026-09-20 a DSP module and its selftest exist, but no EEGDex app, bundle or hand-authored registry is built, and the adapter layer routes EEG files to an EEGDSP that resolves to undefined in every shipped bundle. This guide documents the manifest's intended metrics, not measured behaviour. Consumer dry-electrode EEG (4-channel Muse) has higher artifact rates and lower spatial resolution than clinical PSG. Contact quality gates every downstream metric.
